Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees may submit expense reimbursement claims within 30 days of the expense date. All claims must include valid receipts. Alcohol-related expenses will not be reimbursed. Travel expenses must be pre-approved by the reporting manager. Reimbursements are processed within 10 business days after approval.

Travel Advance Policy

Employees can request a travel advance for official business travel. The advance request must be submitted at least 5 working days before travel. Any unused amount must be returned within 7 days after travel completion.

Payroll Queries

Salary is credited on the last working day of each month. If salary is not credited, employees should verify bank details in the HR portal and contact the payroll team. Salary slips are available in the employee self-service portal.

Tax Deduction Information

Tax deductions are calculated based on declared investment proofs. Employees must submit tax declarations before the financial year deadline. Failure to submit proofs will result in higher tax deduction as per government rules.

Invoice Payment Status

Vendors can check invoice payment status by emailing finance@company.com
 with invoice number and date. Standard payment cycle is 30 days from invoice approval date.

Purchase Request Process

All purchase requests must be submitted through the procurement system. Approval from department head is mandatory. Finance approval is required for purchases exceeding the approved budget limit.

Budget Approval

Departments must operate within approved annual budgets. Any additional budget requirement must be justified and approved by senior management before expense commitment.

Refund Policy

Refunds for official payments will be processed after verification of original payment proof. Refund processing time is 7 to 14 business days.
