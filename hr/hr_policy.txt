Sample HR Policy Document

Company Name: ABC Technologies Pvt Ltd
Effective Date: 1 January 2026

1. Working Hours Policy

1.1 Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
1.2 Employees are required to complete 40 hours per week.
1.3 Flexible working hours are allowed between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM start time with manager approval.
1.4 Overtime must be pre-approved by the reporting manager.

2. Leave Policy
2.1 Annual Leave

Employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Unused annual leave can be carried forward up to 10 days to the next year.

Leave beyond 10 days will lapse.

2.2 Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 12 days of sick leave per year.

Medical certificate required if sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days.

2.3 Casual Leave

Employees are entitled to 6 days of casual leave per year.

Casual leave cannot be carried forward.

2.4 Maternity Leave

Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave as per applicable law.

2.5 Paternity Leave

Male employees are entitled to 10 days of paid paternity leave.

3. Remote Work Policy

3.1 Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
3.2 Fully remote roles must be approved by HR and department head.
3.3 Employees must be available online during core hours: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

4. Probation Policy

4.1 New employees will be on probation for 6 months.
4.2 During probation, either party may terminate employment with 15 days’ notice.
4.3 Confirmation will be provided in writing after successful completion.

5. Notice Period Policy

5.1 Confirmed employees must serve 60 days’ notice upon resignation.
5.2 The company may waive notice period at its discretion.
5.3 Salary in lieu of notice may be accepted.

6. Code of Conduct

6.1 Employees must maintain professional behavior at all times.
6.2 Harassment, discrimination, or misconduct will result in disciplinary action.
6.3 Confidential company information must not be disclosed.

7. Performance Review Policy

7.1 Performance reviews will be conducted annually in March.
7.2 Salary revisions are based on performance ratings and company performance.
7.3 Promotions are not guaranteed and depend on business needs.

8. Travel & Expense Policy

8.1 Travel must be pre-approved by the reporting manager.
8.2 Economy class airfare must be booked for domestic travel.
8.3 Reimbursements must be submitted within 15 days of expense.
8.4 Alcohol expenses will not be reimbursed.

9. IT & Data Security Policy

9.1 Employees must use company-issued devices for official work.
9.2 Sharing passwords is strictly prohibited.
9.3 Data breaches must be reported within 24 hours to IT department.

10. Termination Policy

10.1 Employment may be terminated due to misconduct, poor performance, or business restructuring.
10.2 Final settlement will be processed within 30 days from last working day.
